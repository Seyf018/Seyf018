# Hi, I'm Seifeddine

M.Sc. researcher at [ÉTS Montréal](https://www.etsmtl.ca/), working on decision support that combines **mixed-integer programming** and **machine learning**.

My thesis plans spraying and harvesting against spruce budworm outbreaks in Gaspésie, Quebec: a joint optimization model with a Fix-and-Dive heuristic (manuscript under review), and an interpretable PPO policy for joint spraying and harvesting in development.
